# Research dataset provenance

Andrea Di Martino and Alessandro Amorosi (2023), Sedimentary Facies Analysis and Segmentation, University of Bologna, DOI 10.6092/unibo/amsacta/7308.

https://amsacta.unibo.it/id/eprint/7308/

Source licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. Cropping, exact-palette conversion and Russian class translations are modifications. Preserve attribution and noncommercial/share-alike restrictions. This prepared set is a separate research experiment; do not automatically deploy into commercial CV-1 weights.

The original publisher train/validation/test folders were regrouped by S-prefix before this research experiment. Original publisher Test images occur in new train/val. Only the prepared source-group test is reserved and its pixels were not opened. S-prefixes are grouping proxies, not verified borehole IDs. No depths were inferred.
